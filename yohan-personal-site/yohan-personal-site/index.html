About

I'm a Computer Engineering student at Batangas State University – Alangilan, specializing in Artificial Intelligence. My work sits between embedded systems, AI/ML, and web development — I like taking something from a breadboard to a deployed product, and I like doing it with a team.

Outside coursework, most of my time goes to building ASTRA Developers, a student-led technology organization I founded to give students at BatStateU an earlier, more practical way into the industry. I also serve across BatStateU's student government — from the Supreme Student Council to the College of Engineering Student Council — where I keep landing on the projects that involve building something from nothing: constitutions, platforms, events, teams.

ASTRA Developers

Founder & CEO — A.Y. 2026–2027

A student-led technology organization at BatStateU Alangilan, built on an exclusive partnership with Google Developer Groups. Its motto — Ad Astra per Aspera — is also its operating premise: the infrastructure comes before the events.

What I built first

Before ASTRA ran a single event, it needed to survive past me. I wrote its full Constitution and By-Laws, stood up a Discord platform with a structured membership-verification system, an always-open Technology Support Hub, and a member-only freelance marketplace.

Where it stands now

ASTRA is officially recognized on campus. We ran our first public event, STARBOUND, in June 2026, and our post-recognition orientation, Orbiting the Stars, follows in September 2026 — built to introduce ASTRA on its own terms this time.
